I suspect quite a few people here (though I can’t see the comments (perhaps I should add that feature!) find themselves stuck in a recurring cycle, month after month:

- Morning: Wake up, turn off the alarm, have a quick, light meal, and head out the door for work
- At the workplace: Stare at a computer screen, take a lunch break at noon, then return to staring at the screen again at 1:00 PM
- Evening: Lug a backpack containing a laptop back home
- Night: Do a bit more work, then go to bed once night falls

It’s a cycle that can be truly exhausting, yet it serves as our primary source of income—something we have to endure if we want to earn a living

Life really isn’t as rosy as we imagine, is it?
